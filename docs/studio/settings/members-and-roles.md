# Members & Roles

Studio is multi-user. You invite teammates and give each one a **role** that controls what they can do. Manage it all under **Settings → Members**.

## Roles

Every member has one of four roles:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Read-only. See shows, controllers, media, and rules — but can't change or run anything. |
| **Editor** | Build and run the show: create and edit shows, lighting, audio, and Logic Rules; upload media; provision and configure controllers; deploy; drive Manual Control and Live Preview; update firmware. Can't invite members or change settings. |
| **Admin** | Everything an Editor can do, plus manage the team (invite/remove members, change roles) and the Studio's settings (WiFi, audit log, IP allow-list, billing). |
| **Owner** | Full control, including ownership-level actions for the Studio. A Studio always has at least one Owner. |

Only **Admins** and **Owners** can invite or manage members and open the settings pages.

## Invite a teammate

1. Go to **Settings → Members → Invitations**.
2. Enter the teammate's **email address** and pick a **role**.
3. Click **Send Invite**.
4. Copy the invitation link Studio generates and send it to them (email, chat, however you like).
5. The link expires in **7 days** — if it lapses, just send a new one.

Pending invitations appear in the **Invitations** tab, where you can copy the link again or cancel it. Once accepted, the person shows up in the **Members** list.

## Manage members

In the **Members** list you can change a member's role or remove them, from the menu next to their name. A few guardrails:

- A Studio must always have at least one **Owner** and one **Admin** — you can't remove or demote the last one.
- Removing a member revokes their access immediately.

## Member limits

Your plan includes a set number of members (shown at the top of the Members tab). If you reach the limit, you'll be prompted to upgrade before you can invite more.
