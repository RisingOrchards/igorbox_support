We're excited to announce that we've added waveforms to the audio triggers in the timeline on IgorBox! This new feature will allow you to see the audio waveform of your audio files directly on the timeline, making it easier to edit your show to align with your audio. (We have some new features coming soon that will make this even more useful, so stay tuned!)

<!-- truncate-->

This is a direct result of feedback from our beta testers, so thank you to everyone who has been providing feedback! We're always looking for ways to improve IgorBox and make it even better for you, so please keep the feedback coming! (https://feedback.igorbox.com)
