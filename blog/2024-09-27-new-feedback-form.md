Today we're happy to announce a new feedback form that will allow you to send us feedback directly from the IgorBox web interface. This is a great way to let us know about any issues you're having, suggest new features, or just tell us how much you love IgorBox!

<!-- truncate -->

Starting today, you'll see a new "Feedback" button in the bottom right corner of the IgorBox web interface. If you click on this button, a form will pop up that you can use to send us your feedback.

![Feedback Button](/img/blog/feedback-button.png)

The form is pretty simple. You just choose if you're suggesting a feature or reporting a bug and enter your message. That's it! When you click "submit", the data is sent into our staff chat on Discord and a notification is sent to our team.

![Feedback Form](/img/blog/suggestion.png)

We will capture the page that you are submitting feedback from and include that in the message so we can better understand the context of your feedback. So feel free to send us feedback from anywhere in the IgorBox web interface with your suggestions on how we can make that page you're on better!

Once we have had a chance to evaluate your feedback, we'll add it to our feedback tracker at [feedback.igorbox.com](https://feedback.igorbox.com) and you'll be able to see the status of your feedback there as we work on it.

We hope you enjoy this new feature and we can't wait to hear from you! If you have any questions or feedback about the feedback form, feel free to reach out to us at [feedback.igorbox.com](https://feedback.igorbox.com) or directly on Discord at [discord.igorbox.com](https://discord.igorbox.com). We're always happy to help and we love hearing from you!
